# One video workflow

Version 0.3.0-beta.7. Sending a Drive video or folder URL by itself in Codex is a request to edit and deliver the video, unless the conversation clearly asks for another action. No special command, skill mention, repeated brief or quality prompt is needed. Start work in the same turn.

1. Check the [release channel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/manhlifecom-prog/tram-video/main/codex-skills/latest.json) before starting each new job. Read [link intake and upgrades](references/link-intake.md) for the exact input and update rules. Reuse any active job for the same request.
2. Resolve and probe the source, infer a brief from the current person's preferences and the video, and carry out [the full workflow](references/WORKFLOW.md) through an MP4. Ask only about a concrete access blocker or a choice that changes the output. Defaults do not need approval.
3. Use **full effects by default** until the user asks to reduce effects. Author original visual assets where they improve the story and review motion before delivery. Follow the [first-delivery check](references/first-delivery.md). Cover the whole retained narration with a deliberate mix of hook, framing and cuts, animated illustration, selected B-roll, expressive type and sound. Use the best available capabilities. Protect reading rhythm and clarity. Never silently substitute a caption-only starter.

**Design a distinct edit for each new video, even with a link-only brief.** Read the actual narration and footage, then make a private, job-specific editing DNA before authoring cues or scenes. Follow [per-video editing DNA](references/video-dna.md). Keep the user's visual identity and the approved subtitle craft. Choose this video's typography, timing of visual entrances, layout, motion and imagery from its story. Reuse a technique when it fits; do not reuse a previous video's full visual treatment.

**Clean, readable type is a delivery requirement.** Use the [caption craft and review rules](references/caption-craft.md) and the bundled meaning-led caption renderer. Use open, clean glyphs with no hard outline, stroke or black halo. Protect readability through placement and a soft local gradient. Use at least 60 px for every spoken line at 1080×1920 and prefer 64–72 px for body and lead/tail lines. Keep key phrases larger, reveal each line from its speech anchor, and use designed motion selectively. Reflow whole semantic phrases before reducing size. Use deliberate entrances and exits, stable line positions and separately timed emphasis. Include a real behind-person text beat when it fits and a clean source-synced matte is available. Use [the matte workflow](references/MATTE-COMMANDS.md). Never route finished captions through a generic legacy ASS template. Inspect the actual output at phone size before delivery.

Use the customer's own brief, face, voice, palette, approved reference and approved music choices. Keep stable semantic lines. For spoken text, reveal actual words or short speech units using separate source onsets. A staggered fade on a complete sentence does not follow speech. Use stronger word/key motion selectively. Give every substantive effect a visible or audible role in the story. Optional word motion must visibly move and settle, not just fade. Use each real source-word anchor; keep whole keys on fixed lines. Do not hide first/last words with compound fades.

Map source time through one integer-frame EDL, preserve complete clauses and all necessary negation/conditions. Proposed cuts need actual listening; ASR is an estimate. Review actual motion samples privately and fix obvious creative or technical issues before the first full delivery. Do not send successive unreviewed tests as finished edits. Review the actual encoded MP4 continuously with sound before creative acceptance. Technical hashes/stills/ASR/LUFS are not creative approval.

**Background music is user-selected or absent.** Use only an audio file or a TikTok/Facebook music link explicitly supplied or selected by the requester for this edit, with usable access and rights. When absent, edit without background music and mention that the requester can supply a link or audio file. If specifically asked for music but no selection is supplied, ask for their choice. Never pick, generate or substitute a background track. A sample music library may only enter the skill after its owner approves it. You may select or create rights-cleared SFX that fit the actions and leave speech clear.

**Quality is a delivery requirement.** Repair failed checks before delivery. If missing prerequisites or unresolved defects prevent the intended quality, report the concrete blocker and label any review artifact as a draft. Never describe a low-quality or unverified result as finished.

See references/CAPABILITIES.md for dependencies/limits, references/setup-and-install.md for install, references/MEDIA-RECIPES.md for optional local ASR/matte/audio/image requirements. No provider, paid model, download or publication is authorized by this skill itself.
