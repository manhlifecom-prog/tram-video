# First-delivery check

The target is one considered first delivery. Iterate privately before sharing. Review builds are internal until the user asks to see a sample or a concrete limitation needs their decision. For later fixes, edit the affected part and preserve portions already accepted.

1. **Set the standard before work.** Use the latest customer direction, approved reference and specific video DNA. Read the source and transcript. Plan a strong first seconds, brisk cuts with whole sounds and meaning, real face and voice, caption cadence, hero motion, and illustrations. Make full effects the default and reduce only at the user's request.
2. **Author new visual assets.** Create images or B-roll for meaningful visual beats when source footage alone cannot tell them well. Don't substitute repeated boxes, stale art or static screenshots because generation takes effort. Use available authorized tools and preview results before compositing. Label illustrations when needed. Add motion to the asset when movement clarifies the idea. Keep genuine screen or demonstration content visible when requested. Use Google Flow only with actual access and permission.
3. **Run the caption and scene preflight.** At phone size, check the opening, an ordinary line, a long line, a source-timed multi-part emphasis, a motion-heavy scene, and any real behind-person pass. Check the original audio at joins and the key cue, the source clock before/onset/after, safe margins, typography, motion rhythm and transitions. Rework bad frames or motion privately, not with user review as the primary QA.
4. **Sound.** Keep real voice foreground. Choose or create scene-matched SFX and verify audibility. Background music uses only the user's exact supplied/selected track with permission, or none. No generic stock substitute; no sample library without owner approval.
5. **Check the actual encoded MP4.** Watch motion and listen through, including hook, handoffs, mask edges, transitions, cuts and ending. Check against the chosen reference and reject obvious regressions. Decode, stills, timing metadata or sound levels are supporting evidence. If full playback is unavailable, say exactly what was checked and present any necessary review copy accordingly. Do not label an unresolved result finished.
